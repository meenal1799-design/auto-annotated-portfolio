## The challenge

Cloud migration assessments can require weeks of manual analysis before teams
can compare costs, build a business case, and decide what to migrate. The
experience needed to help users move from a complex infrastructure estate to
clear, actionable decisions.

## Product experience

I designed a conversational approach to total cost of ownership (TCO) modeling
for migrations from on-premises environments to AWS, Google Cloud (GCP), or
Microsoft Azure, as well as cloud-to-cloud moves. The experience guides teams
from describing their environment and analyzing infrastructure assets through
comparing migration scenarios and generating a business case.

## Multi-agent architecture

I designed a distributed orchestrator-worker architecture in which multiple
agents collaborate across the transformation journey. The system analyzes
10,000+ infrastructure assets simultaneously, supports TCO assessment and
business-case creation, and carries the workflow forward toward migration
planning and execution.

## Outcomes

- Reduced cloud migration assessment effort by 70% through agentic analysis.
- Cut TCO assessment creation from 2–6 weeks to less than 24 hours.
- Accelerated customer onboarding 5x through the platform and AWS partnership.

The work combined conversational product design, agent orchestration, and
enterprise cloud transformation to help teams make migration decisions faster.
